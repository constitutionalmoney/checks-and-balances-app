# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in the Checks & Balances Protocol community requires respectful, evidence-led, privacy-conscious conduct.

Expected behaviour includes:

- discussing ideas and code rather than attacking people;
- separating verified facts, proposals, assumptions, and personal opinions;
- treating unfinished protocol questions as unfinished;
- respecting political, cultural, legal, technical, and personal differences;
- protecting personal information and security-sensitive details;
- giving credit for others’ work;
- accepting correction when evidence changes; and
- helping newcomers contribute without lowering safety or quality standards.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, discrimination, or targeted humiliation;
- doxxing or publishing identity documents, addresses, private evidence, credentials, or confidential communications;
- pressuring a person to disclose political views as a condition of verification or contribution;
- impersonating a participant, committee member, maintainer, organization, or official deployment;
- knowingly presenting planned or experimental functionality as operational;
- publishing vulnerabilities before coordinated disclosure;
- manipulating committees, renewal selection, civic signals, or contribution records; and
- using project spaces to promote unrelated illegal, fraudulent, violent, or exploitative activity.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, project chat, community events, verification-pilot activities, and public conduct where someone is acting as a project representative.
